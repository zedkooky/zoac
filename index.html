(02) Safari Types

Choose Your
Kind of Safari

Five ways to be in the wild. Each changes what you see, what you hear and how close you get.

01 Walking Safaris Slow, quiet and close to the ground. The purest way to read the bush, pioneered in Zambia’s Luangwa Valley.
Walking Safaris
02 Canoe & River Safaris Drift past elephants at eye level. Rivers like the Zambezi and the Okavango reveal what roads never reach.
Canoe & River Safaris
03 Game Drives The classic, done well: an open vehicle, a patient guide and the freedom to follow a story as it unfolds.
Game Drives
04 Mobile Safaris Camps that move with the season and the animals. Simple canvas, real remoteness, nothing between you and the night.
Mobile Safaris
05 Luxury Wilderness Exceptional comfort in genuinely wild places — where the design serves the landscape, not the other way round.

(06) Editor’s Recommendation · Zambia

One Destination We Know Exceptionally Well

Opposite Mana Pools, the Zambezi slows and spreads into channels and wooded islands. Elephants wade between them at dusk. We return here more than anywhere else — by canoe, on foot, and to camps that keep their numbers small.

If you want to see what safari becomes when it is built around a river, start here.

Best months
May – October
Getting there
40 min from Lusaka
Best by
Canoe, on foot
