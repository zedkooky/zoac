Safari Destinations Worth Knowing

Six countries, six very different ways of being in the wild.

On this page

  1. Start in Zambia
  2. Beyond Zambia
  3. How they compare

There is no single best safari country for everyone — but if we had to choose one, it would be Zambia. Here is why, and how it compares with the five other countries we know best.

Start in Zambia

Zambia is our home ground and, we think, Africa’s most complete safari country: the birthplace of the walking safari in South Luangwa, canoeing on the Lower Zambezi, the best side of Victoria Falls, the vast Kafue, two great migrations, tiger fishing and Lake Tanganyika — with far fewer visitors than its neighbours.

Beyond Zambia

Each of our other countries does something brilliantly. Many of them combine easily with Zambia.

For your first trip, combining two countries is easy — Zambia with Zimbabwe’s Mana Pools, or Botswana’s Chobe with Victoria Falls from Livingstone, for example. See when to go and what it costs to narrow it down.

Where to go

The countries

01

Zambia

Africa’s most complete safari

Luangwa · Lower Zambezi · Victoria Falls · Kafue · Tanganyika

The birthplace of the walking safari, the best side of Victoria Falls, migrations, great lakes and tiger fishing.

Zambia
02

Botswana

Low volume, high wilderness

Okavango Delta · Chobe · Central Kalahari

A delta that floods in the dry season, protected by a model that keeps visitor numbers deliberately low.

Botswana
03

Tanzania

Scale beyond reckoning

Serengeti · Ruaha · Nyerere

From the great migration to the remote, rarely visited south — a country of extraordinary range.

Tanzania
04

Kenya

The conservancy model

Maasai Mara · Laikipia · Samburu

Community-owned conservancies are quietly rewriting how a safari can work, and who it benefits.

Kenya
05

Namibia

Desert and silence

Etosha · Damaraland · Skeleton Coast

Desert-adapted wildlife, vast open country and a stillness that stays with you long after.

Namibia
06

Zimbabwe

A guiding heritage

Mana Pools · Hwange · Gonarezhou

Some of Africa’s most respected guides, and far fewer vehicles than its reputation suggests.
